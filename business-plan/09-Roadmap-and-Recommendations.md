# 9. Recommendations & Roadmap — What To Do, In Order

This is the "so what do I actually do on Monday morning" document. It pulls together every "get a specialist for this" flag raised across the package into one checklist, then lays out the sequence.

## 9.1 What this consulting package covers well (lean on it)

- Overall business model, revenue structure, and unit economics logic
- Market sizing, competitive landscape, and customer segmentation (grounded in current, cited 2026 KSA data)
- A realistic budgeting framework (startup costs, running costs, pricing structure, break-even logic)
- Risk identification and mitigation strategy
- The structure and content of your contractor partnership program and SLAs
- A clear, phased, capital-efficient rollout sequence
- A checklist of exactly which licensed professionals you need, for what, and roughly when

**Use this package as**: the brief you hand to a lawyer, accountant, developer, or bank — it will make every one of those conversations faster and cheaper, because you'll walk in with clear requirements instead of a vague idea.

## 9.2 What this package cannot do — and exactly who to hire instead

| You need... | Because... | Get this specifically |
|---|---|---|
| **A Saudi corporate/commercial lawyer** | To file your CR with the correct activity code, confirm you don't need your own SCA/trade license, draft the Partnership Agreement and Terms of Service/Privacy Policy, and confirm PDPL compliance | A written legal opinion on your licensing classification **before** you spend on app development — this is the single highest-priority external hire |
| **A licensed accountant (Saudi-qualified, ZATCA e-invoicing experienced)** | To register for VAT/Zakat correctly, set up compliant e-invoicing, and build your real 3-statement financial model | Engage before your first revenue, ideally before Stage 0 spending, so your books are clean from day one |
| **An app development agency or CTO** | To actually design, build, and maintain the customer app, contractor app, and admin dashboard | Get 3+ competing quotes; use this package as your Product Requirements brief; ask specifically about their experience with two-sided marketplace / on-demand service apps in the GCC |
| **A SAMA-licensed payment gateway provider** | Payment collection/disbursement is regulated; you cannot build this yourself | Approach 2-3 providers (e.g., established KSA gateways) for integration terms and fees |
| **An insurance broker** | To structure both your own platform liability policy and the insurance requirements you'll impose on contractor partners | Engage once you know your exact operating model (post-legal-opinion) |
| **An HR/labor law consultant** | To model your Nitaqat (Saudization) quota and hiring mix correctly as you build your internal team | Engage when you're finalizing your Stage 1 hiring plan |
| **A business-setup consultancy or MISA specialist (if any founder/investor is non-Saudi)** | Foreign ownership requires a MISA investment license, which has its own timeline | Start this track in parallel with legal work — don't let it become a launch-week surprise |

## 9.3 The recommended sequence

### Phase 0 — Before spending real money (Weeks 1-4)
1. Get the **licensing legal opinion** (§9.2) — this one answer shapes almost everything downstream.
2. Start informal **contractor conversations** in Jeddah — gauge real interest and commission tolerance before you build anything.
3. Stand up a **simple landing page + WhatsApp Business** presence — start capturing interest immediately, at near-zero cost.

### Stage 0 — Lean Validation (Months 1-3, ~SAR 80,000-120,000)
4. File your **CR and Maroof** registration.
5. Sign your **first 5-10 contractor partners** in Jeddah across all four trade categories, using a lawyer-reviewed simple agreement.
6. Run real bookings manually (a person taking WhatsApp/phone requests and dispatching by phone) for 6-8 weeks.
7. **Measure honestly**: booking conversion rate, contractor reliability, actual customer satisfaction, real cost per acquired customer. If these numbers are discouraging, this is the cheapest possible point to learn that and adjust the model — far better than discovering it after a SAR 600,000 app build.

### Stage 1 — Full Buildout (Months 3-18, ~SAR 900,000-1,300,000)
8. Commission the **full app build** (customer + contractor + admin) with a development partner, using this package as the brief.
9. Complete **legal documentation** (Partnership Agreement, ToS, Privacy Policy), **payment gateway integration**, and **insurance** setup.
10. **Launch publicly in Jeddah** with the full app, timed ideally before the summer AC-demand season.
11. Build out the team per the [Financial Plan](06-Financial-Plan.md) staffing ramp.
12. **Expand to Riyadh** (~month 7) once Jeddah's contractor density, SLA compliance, and dispatch process are proven stable.
13. **Expand to Dammam** (~month 10) on the same proof-first basis.
14. Introduce the **"Portal Care" subscription plan** once you have enough completed jobs and trust signal (ratings, repeat customers) to sell recurring value convincingly — typically once you're a few months into Jeddah operations.

### Phase 2 — Consolidate & extend (Months 18+)
15. Evaluate expansion to **Madinah, Taif, Khobar, Jubail** based on which Phase 1 city performed best and what that tells you about market characteristics that predict success.
16. Evaluate **corporate/facility client** expansion (see [Business Model](02-Business-Model.md) §2.6) once residential operations are stable and profitable.
17. Revisit whether any high-volume, high-margin service line is worth **selectively bringing in-house** (own technicians) rather than staying 100% partner-based — only once you have the data to know which lines would actually benefit.

## 9.4 A one-page decision checklist for your very next step

Before you spend a single riyal beyond Phase 0:

- [ ] Written legal opinion obtained on licensing classification
- [ ] At least 3 contractor companies per trade have expressed real interest in Jeddah
- [ ] Landing page/WhatsApp channel live and generating at least some inbound interest
- [ ] Stage 0 budget (SAR 80,000-120,000) secured and ring-fenced
- [ ] A named person (you, or a co-founder/early hire) owns operations/dispatch day-to-day — this cannot be a part-time afterthought

If all five are true, proceed to Stage 0. If any are not, that's your actual next task — not app development.

## 9.5 Closing note

The idea is sound and the market is real and growing — but it is not empty, and the businesses that win categories like this one usually win on **operational discipline** (contractor quality, SLA reliability, honest unit economics) rather than on having the flashiest app. Everything in this package is built around that principle: prove it lean, fund it in stages, and treat the licensing and contractor-quality questions as seriously as the technology.
