# Maintenance Portal — Business Plan Package

Welcome. This folder is your complete, plain-English business package for **Maintenance Portal**: an app (and/or website) that connects residential clients in Saudi Arabia with existing, already-staffed maintenance contractors (electrical, plumbing, HVAC, and minor civil finishes), starting in Jeddah, Riyadh, and Dammam, with a path to Madinah, Taif, Khobar, and Jubail — and later, corporate clients.

Every document is written the way a consultant would explain it to you over coffee — no jargon left unexplained, no walls of text without a table or a list to break it up.

## A note on how to use this

This package is a **strategic and operational business plan** — the kind a management consultant delivers. It is grounded in real, current (2026) Saudi market data and regulatory bodies (sourced and cited in the Market Research and Feasibility documents). It is **not** a substitute for:

- A licensed Saudi corporate lawyer (for the actual contracts, licenses, and filings)
- A Saudi Contractors Authority (SCA)–licensed engineer (for technical/safety sign-off on electrical, plumbing, and HVAC standards)
- A registered accountant/auditor (for tax filing and statutory books)

Every place this matters, the relevant document tells you clearly: *"this part, this plan can do — that part, you need a licensed X, and here's how to get one."* Document 9 pulls all of these into a single checklist.

All figures are in **Saudi Riyal (SAR)** unless stated otherwise (1 USD ≈ 3.75 SAR, the pegged rate). Figures are planning-grade estimates based on comparable businesses and current market data, not quotes — treat them as a budgeting framework to validate with real supplier quotes before you commit money.
