# 1. Executive Summary

## The idea, in one paragraph

Maintenance Portal is a booking and dispatch platform (app first, website as a backup channel) that lets homeowners and tenants in Saudi Arabia request electrical, plumbing, HVAC (air conditioning), and minor civil finishing (paint, tiling) work with a few taps, and get a **vetted technician from an existing, already-staffed maintenance contractor** at their door — with an upfront price estimate, a tracked job, and a guarantee. We do not hire technicians ourselves. We partner with maintenance companies that already have technicians stationed in Jeddah, Riyadh, and Dammam, and route work to them. We make money by taking a **commission on every completed job** plus a **small booking fee**, and later, a subscription for recurring maintenance (like an annual AC service plan).

## Why this, why now

- Saudi Arabia's residential market is large and growing: Riyadh's metro area is home to roughly 8 million people, Jeddah roughly 4.7 million, and the Dammam–Khobar–Dhahran conurbation roughly 1.4 million — a combined addressable population north of 14 million in the first three cities alone.
- Vision 2030's housing programs have driven a construction and homeownership boom, which means a growing base of owner-occupied homes that will need ongoing maintenance for decades.
- The market is not empty — apps like MAHA, HomeXA, Fix Riyadh Now, Ajeer, and Noon's home-services vertical already operate here (see [Market Research](03-Market-Research.md)) — but it is **fragmented and inconsistent**: no single player has dominant, trusted, multi-city coverage across electrical + plumbing + HVAC + civil finishes with real SLA guarantees. That fragmentation is the opening.
- The **"partner, don't hire"** model is the right one to start with: it means near-zero technician payroll, no vehicle fleet, no tool inventory, and a launch that can happen in months, not years — provided the partner agreements and quality controls (this package covers both) are done properly.

## What makes this different from "just another app"

1. **We don't compete with contractors, we plug into them.** Existing maintenance companies already have technicians, vans, tools, and Civil-Defense/municipal licensing in these cities. We bring them customers and a professional booking/dispatch/payment layer; they bring the labor and the legal trade licenses.
2. **One app, every trade.** Most competitors specialize (AC-only, or plumbing-only, or a single city). A resident with a leaking pipe today and a broken AC next month should not need four different apps.
3. **A real SLA, not just a phone call.** Response-time and quality guarantees, backed by a deposit/holdback mechanism on contractors and a customer-facing rating and refund policy (detail in [SLA Templates](08-SLA-Templates.md)).

## What this requires from you, honestly

- **Capital**: we recommend a two-stage approach — a **lean validation stage** costing **SAR 80,000–120,000** to prove real demand before big spend, followed by a **full 3-city buildout** (app, team, launch, and runway to break-even) costing **SAR 900,000 – 1,300,000** (~USD 240,000–347,000) — only committed once Stage 0 shows real signal. Financing options (self-funded, angel, or the government-backed **Kafalah** SME loan-guarantee program) are covered in [Financial Plan](06-Financial-Plan.md).
- **Licensing you cannot skip**: a Commercial Registration (CR) for a platform/marketplace activity, Maroof e-commerce verification, a SAMA-regulated payment gateway, and — critically — making sure every contractor you onboard already holds their own valid municipal (Balady) and, where applicable, Civil Defense and SCA trade licenses. **We do not need our own contracting license if we never touch the tools ourselves** — but this must be confirmed in writing with a Saudi corporate lawyer before launch (see [Feasibility Study](04-Feasibility-Study.md)).
- **A lean start, not a big-bang app launch.** Our strongest recommendation (detailed in [Recommendations & Roadmap](09-Roadmap-and-Recommendations.md)) is to prove demand and contractor reliability with a lightweight booking flow (simple site + WhatsApp Business + a small human dispatch team) for 2–3 months **before** spending the bulk of the tech budget on a polished native app.

## The 12-month goal

Launch in Jeddah first (largest addressable base, high smartphone/app usage, strong existing home-services competition to learn from), prove the model with 8–12 contractor partners and a few hundred completed jobs, then expand to Riyadh and Dammam once the SLA and partner-payment mechanics are proven. Monthly operating break-even at the 3-city level is modeled at roughly **month 10-12**, with full payback of the initial capital around **month 18-24** (see [Financial Plan](06-Financial-Plan.md) for the full model and its assumptions).
